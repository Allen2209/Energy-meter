Smart Prepaid Energy Monitoring and Theft Detection System

Abstract & Objectives

This project implements a modern IoT-based electricity billing solution designed to replace traditional post-paid billing while simultaneously addressing electricity distribution loss caused by energy theft.

Utilizing an **ESP32 Microcontroller** interfaced with **PZEM-004T AC energy metering sensors**, the system tracks real-time Voltage, Current, Active Power, and cumulative Energy consumption. Telemetry is streamed securely to an EMQX cloud broker via **MQTT over WebSockets**.
